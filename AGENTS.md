# Public profile maintenance

This repository presents a personal learning and building practice through business functions. Keep the profile grounded, concise, and useful to readers.

- Describe private work by function, methods, and generalized deliverables. Do not use customer, partner, or client names, even when they appear in accessible repositories or task records. Public open-source repositories may be named only when the user explicitly asks to feature them.
- Do not publish client identities, private links, local paths, contact information, internal screenshots, data samples, proprietary workflows, or unverified performance claims.
- The confidentiality rule covers Chinese and English text, image pixels, alternative text, filenames, rendering source, comments, metadata, and commit messages.
- Keep the animation and its static alternative aligned with the README. Re-render both after changing their labels; inspect the result before publishing.
- Keep the animation's Chinese and English editions aligned. README.md uses the zh images; README.en.md and the collapsed English section use the en images. Each picture's reduced-motion source must use the same language as its animation.
- Publish only the intended profile files. Keep local research, source-to-project mappings, history backups, and review contact sheets outside this repository.
- Inspect the current remote state before writing and preserve unrelated work. Do not restore content from older, unsanitized profile copies.
- Retain reduced-motion sources and static-image alternatives for the profile motion and all featured project motion.
- Maintain visible language links at the top: Chinese is the profile default in README.md, and English opens README.en.md.
- Update Chinese and English together. README.zh.md mirrors the Chinese default for existing links; README.en.md contains the complete English edition. Keep a short collapsed English introduction on the Chinese page.
- Use a direct first-person voice, concrete work, and clearly stated judgments. Keep the nine business functions. Do not copy another creator's signature wording, achievements, or identity.
- Lead with Eric's broader work across business, products, design, and learning. The four game Skills are one compact, collapsed example, not the profile's central identity. Keep project artwork compact and use everyday language before technical terminology.

Confidentiality is the default for future updates; access to project material is not permission to disclose it on the public profile.
